# Build Roadmap

## V0.1.5 — foundation (current)
Terminal shell, API contracts, provider abstraction, risk calculation, tests, infrastructure.

## V0.2 — market data
Provider adapters, WebSocket ingestion, tick normalization, candle builder, TimescaleDB persistence, reconnect logic, data-quality checks.

## V0.3 — charting and scanner
Candlestick chart, multi-timeframe candles, watchlist scanner, ATR/volatility, session analytics.

## V0.4 — XAUUSD intelligence
HTF bias, market structure, liquidity, FVG/OB, premium/discount, volatility regime, DXY/yields/correlation features.

## V0.5 — research lab
Event-driven backtest, spread/slippage model, walk-forward, Monte Carlo, parameter sensitivity, experiment registry.

## V0.6 — AI research
Tool-calling research agent, evidence ledger, strategy hypothesis generation, backtest tool, report generation.

## V0.7 — paper trading
Order simulator, fills, positions, P&L, journal, risk limits.

## V0.8 — MT5 bridge
Separate execution adapter, order reconciliation, kill switch, audit log, paper/live environment separation.

## V1.0 — production terminal
Authentication, multi-user tenancy, billing, monitoring, alerting, permissions, deployment hardening.
